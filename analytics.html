How these numbers are counted

Page views count successful requests for the welcome page, timeline, and research entries. They are not unique people or sessions. Refreshes count again. Signed-in owner visits, browsers with the owner exclusion preference, private pages, known bots, and prefetch requests are excluded; other automated traffic may remain. Older anonymous owner visits cannot be identified or removed from the aggregates.

Traffic is stored as daily totals by page and referring domain, with separate totals by device type. No IP addresses, account identifiers, full referral URLs, or raw user agents are stored. A secure browser cookie remembers only the owner exclusion preference, not a visitor identifier. Referrers may be withheld by browsers. Device types are estimates; browsers using desktop mode may report a tablet as desktop. Existing comments and suggestions are counted from their submission dates.

Google search queries are aggregated Search Console results and cannot be tied to a particular visitor or page-view record. Google may omit low-volume queries, and recent data can be delayed.
