How these numbers are counted

Page views count successful requests for the welcome page, timeline, and research entries. They are not unique people or sessions. Refreshes count again. Signed-in owner visits, private pages, known bots, and prefetch requests are excluded; other automated traffic may remain.

Traffic is stored as daily totals by page and referring domain. No IP addresses, account identifiers, full referral URLs, or analytics cookies are stored. Referrers may be withheld by browsers. Earlier traffic is unavailable; existing comments and suggestions are counted from their submission dates.
